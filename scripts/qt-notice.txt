You may replace the Qt libraries with interface-compatible modified builds.
On Windows, extract the ZIP, replace the Qt DLLs in bin and the matching
plugins in plugins. Preserve the layout and leave bin/qt.conf in place.
For a direct macOS download, copy FreezeCut.app to a writable location, replace the frameworks
in Contents/Frameworks and matching plugins in Contents/PlugIns, and re-sign
each changed framework or plugin and the app with `codesign --force --sign -`.
Re-sign the nested code before the app. macOS may request Screen Recording
permission again for the modified app.

For a Mac App Store build, choose Open Source Licenses in the FreezeCut menu bar
menu, then Get Source Code. It links to the exact FreezeCut and Qt sources for
this build. Follow the release guide to rebuild an independent local app with
your modified Qt SDK and run it with local signing. You may copy, modify, and
redistribute the Qt libraries under the terms of their LGPL license.
