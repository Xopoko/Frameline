# FreezeCut privacy policy

FreezeCut records a screenshot when you start a capture. On macOS, screen capture requires your permission in System Settings. The app stores original screenshots, thumbnails, and capture details such as the time, type, dimensions, and device label in your local Library. It also keeps your preferences on this device. You can copy or export images, and you can turn off automatic copying in Settings.

FreezeCut has no account, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated screenshot service. We do not receive your screenshots, settings, or usage data. The app sends library data only when you configure a connection or sharing. If you enable automatic uploads, FreezeCut can queue and send screenshots to the library you choose. That library may be hosted on your own computer or on a server you trust. Keep connection codes and tokens private. The operator of a library you connect to controls the data stored there.

The local Library keeps captures until you remove them. To remove local captures, quit FreezeCut and remove the corresponding folders from its `history` directory. For the Mac App Store version, find that directory in Finder under `~/Library/Containers/io.github.xopoko.Frameline/Data/Library/Application Support/FreezeCut/.frameline/`. Direct downloads use `~/.frameline/`. Exported copies and copies in a shared library are managed separately. You can turn off sharing and automatic uploads in Settings, and revoke Screen Recording permission in macOS System Settings.

For privacy questions, use the [FreezeCut support page](https://github.com/Xopoko/FreezeCut/issues). Please keep personal information and screenshots out of public reports.
